# Explorers-
I have an idea for an app game, where you control a space ship, and crew. Exploring the deep space, with the card deck combat system.

Explorers (working title)
A mobile space-exploration game combining resource management with card-based crew combat.
Concept:
You captain a small rocket exploring an uncharted region of space. Your ship is attacked by raiders known as Ravagers(name might change)— you and one crew member escape into a single compartment and launch, starting the game with almost nothing.
From there, you chart your own course through a fully undiscovered, dark map. As you explore, you find:

Mining sites — gather 3 types of resources
Combat encounters — fight hostile ships or Ravager patrols
Emergency beacons — investigate to recover additional ship compartments and grow your crew
Core Mechanics
Crew
Every crew member has a specific job, with unique perks, HP, and an attack stat. Crew are your central resource: at any time, each one can be assigned to one of:
Repairing/operating the ship
Mining a site
Fighting in combat
A crew member working on the ship cannot simultaneously mine or fight — this creates the core tension of the game: growing your ship and crew always costs you strength somewhere else.
Combat
Combat is card-based. Each crew member is represented as a card, and players build their own deck from their available crew, choosing combinations that synergize well for the fights ahead.
Structure (in progress)
Currently exploring a roguelike run structure with meta-progression: each expedition is a timed run that ends if the ship/crew is lost, but permanent unlocks (new crew types, ship blueprints, card upgrades) carry over between runs.
